Welcome to Ananddham

A 35-Acre Agro Sanctuary For Families & Corporates

Ananddham is designed to give you and your children a genuine taste of Indian agricultural heritage. Walk through organic plantations, taste farm-fresh sugarcane juice, and rest under lush green canopies.

Our Main Attractions

Pool Raindance & Waterfall

Enjoy a clean freshwater swimming pool and a family rain dance with lively music.

Horse Ride & Forest Safari

Traditional open-air safaris to explore our organic orchards and farm perimeter.

Authentic Chulha Food

Hot Pithla-Bhakri, Thecha, and traditional meals prepared fresh using organic vegetables.

Traditional Games

Experience the thrill of Viti-Dandu, Lagori, Archery, and exciting rope-way games.
